Release channels

CoreArmorX is free to download and use. Public stable releases are distributed through the official Modrinth project. Paid memberships do not sell the stable plugin.

Supporter preview builds

Supporters may receive temporary previews, release candidates, staging builds and non-critical hotfix candidates for testing. These builds may be incomplete and are not supporter-exclusive stable releases.

Critical fixes

Confirmed security, exploit, data-loss, major-crash and other critical stability fixes are released publicly when ready. A short preview validation does not create a paid-only fix.

Paid memberships

Membership may include priority support, setup/configuration review, troubleshooting, debug/log analysis, preview channels, roadmap updates and testing participation. It does not unlock commands, modules, gameplay advantages or server-side functionality.

Discord authentication

The authentication bot verifies active Patreon membership and assigns Discord roles for supporter channels. The plugin does not require Patreon authentication.
