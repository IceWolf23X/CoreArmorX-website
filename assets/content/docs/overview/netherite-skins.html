Skin recipes accept clean netherite armor and convert it to a vanilla visual material while applying a netherite-equivalent armor profile. Already-skinned pieces must be stripped before another skin can be applied.

Default skins

Leather, copper, chainmail, iron, gold and diamond skins cover helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots. Leather skins remain compatible with vanilla dye behavior.

Inventory models

REAL_TIER is the default and keeps a netherite inventory model while the equipped appearance follows the visual skin. VISUAL_SKIN shows the visual material icon. CoreArmorX needs no resource pack for either mode.

Lore and placeholders

skins.lore.generated-lines controls the final generated lore. A list entry exactly equal to {item_lore} reinserts the saved original lore; omitting it hides but does not discard the snapshot. Placeholders cover skin, identifiers, clean/visual/real materials, leather color formats and cosmetic durability values.

Cosmetic durability

Modes are DISABLED, GLOBAL, BY_SKIN and BY_ITEM. Damage currently mirrors real item damage with a multiplier. On break, the only supported action is REMOVE_SKIN. Mending can be disabled, split repair points or double the repair into cosmetic durability.

Removal and fire

By default, a skinned piece plus shears returns clean netherite armor and damages the shears by one. Dropped skinned armor is protected from configured fire/lava destruction while stripping is disabled. When enabled, those causes strip the skin instead of destroying the item.
