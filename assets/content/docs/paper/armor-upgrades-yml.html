Each child of upgrades is an independent route.

Definition schema

  • enabled: include the route.
  • from-tier or from-material: source.
  • to-tier or to-material: output.
  • ingredient: Bukkit material or a group from config.yml.
  • cost-mode: VANILLA_RECIPE_COST or CUSTOM.
  • count: positive custom cost.
  • permission: checked when progression locks apply.

Bundled definitions

leather_to_copper, copper_to_chainmail, chainmail_to_iron, iron_to_gold, iron_to_diamond and iron_helmet_to_turtle. Supported tiers are leather, copper, chainmail, iron, gold, diamond and netherite; exact material fields support special one-piece routes.

Validation

Missing endpoints, unknown material/group values and invalid costs disable the affected definition with a warning instead of creating an unsafe recipe.
