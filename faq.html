FAQ

Common questions about CoreArmorX.

Short answers for server owners testing the first MVP.

Does CoreArmorX require a resource pack?

No. The MVP uses vanilla armor materials and works without CustomModelData or a forced pack.

Does styled armor really keep Netherite behavior?

Styled pieces receive Netherite-equivalent armor values, toughness, knockback resistance, and durability. Test a full set on your Paper version before public rollout.

Can players fake a style token by renaming paper?

No. Token identity is stored in PersistentDataContainer. Display name and lore are only presentation.

What item data is preserved?

CoreArmorX preserves names, lore, enchantments, damage, repair cost, trims where supported, item flags, unbreakable state, compatible custom model data, non-CoreArmorX PDC, and non-CoreArmorX attribute modifiers.

Can styles require permissions?

Yes. Enable settings.require-permission-for-styles and assign per-style nodes such as corearmorx.style.diamond.

Are wardrobe GUI and permanent unlocks included?

Not in the first MVP. They are planned follow-up systems once conversion, reset, tokens, recipes, and metadata behavior are stable.

Is CoreCaseX or CoreChatX required?

No. CoreArmorX is CoreX-aligned, but integrations must stay optional.
